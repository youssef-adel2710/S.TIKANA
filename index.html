About the Café

ABOUT S.TIKANA

Beneath swaying palms and strings of amber lantern-light, S.TIKANA was built around one idea: that a night is better shared. This is an outdoor Egyptian oriental café where wooden benches meet bamboo shade, where tea is poured slow and conversation is poured slower.

Every evening the courtyard fills — friends catching up over shisha, cups of Turkish coffee, and the low hum of oriental music drifting between the trees. No rush, no pretense. Just warm light, good drinks, and Egyptian nights that come alive.
